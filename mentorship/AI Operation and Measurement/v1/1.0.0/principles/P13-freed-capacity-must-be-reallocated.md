# P13 · Freed capacity is only value when it is reallocated

**Statement.** Gross time saved by AI is not profit. Human Net Productivity Gain is the net capacity, and it yields financial return only if it is deliberately moved to higher-value work. Leadership owns that reallocation; freed capacity that is not redeployed is a cost, not a gain.

**Why.** "Gross hours saved × average hourly rate" is a fiction: hours saved but spent in meetings, browsing or debugging AI errors create zero economic value. Shifting 5 hrs/week from routine work (~$75/hr) to strategic work (~$200/hr) is worth ~$32,500 per employee per year; the same hours absorbed into the same low-leverage work are worth nothing.

**Rules.**
- Capacity Reallocation Value (CRV) = Human NPG × Reallocation Rate × Value Differential. Net of AI run cost it feeds AI Yield.
- Reallocation Rate is measured, not assumed (the source example assumes 100%; 60% is realistic).
- Keep a reallocation roster (skills, freed hours/week, current team, target initiative) of Capacity Reallocation Resources: cross-trained people who can move to the current bottleneck stage or a new strategic initiative.
- Measure: reallocation rate, time-to-redeploy, % of freed hours landing on roadmap work.
- Send reallocated capacity to the highest-CD3 item or the stage holding the most delay cost.
- Target state cohort is the Augmented Strategist (high time saved, calibrated automated verification, capacity redirected). Example OKR: move 60% of eligible staff into that cohort.
- Portfolio health scorecard: end-to-end lead time (idea → customer); bottleneck stage cycle time + hand-off wait; Verification Tax trend (should fall); Reallocation Rate; CRV vs AI spend; % of AI initiatives moving a business KPI.

**Primitives governed.** CRV, Reallocation Rate, Value Differential, Capacity arbitrage, Capacity Reallocation Resources, reallocation roster, Workforce Productivity Matrix.
