# P16 · Sequence the rollout and budget the tuition

**Statement.** Roll the framework out in four time-boxed team rollout stages, run team by team (or business unit by business unit), that build the Five Pillars toward Business outcome and ROI, each closing with a telemetry-computed exit gate and each unlocking a ceiling on per-agent maturity levels. Each team is measured at its own level; team results roll up to the business unit and the organization, but the team is the focal point. Expect and fund a temporary dip (the Delivery J-curve) as the tuition cost of adoption: an organization-wide investment that each team pays on its own curve.

**Why.** Deploying all six domains at once overwhelms teams when basic telemetry is missing. The initial productivity and stability dip is not a failure of the technology but a budgeted cost of adapting legacy verification systems to agentic output; if it is not planned for, it looks like failure and gets cut.

**Rules.**
- Stage 1 Foundation (months 1–3): pillars Adoption and Governance; unlocks L0–2; exit when Telemetry First criteria are met. Top-down (leadership defines OKRs, SLAs, Total NPG) and bottom-up (teams build Agent Catalog, harness, guardrails, SLIs, baselines) meet in the middle through middle-management scorecards. No more Shadow AI.
- Stage 2 Optimization (months 4–6): pillars Proficiency and Reliability; unlocks L3 (HITL pilots); exit when the verification pipeline is live, Verification Tax is falling and pilots hold SLOs. Moves: step and task SLIs, Isolated Specification Tests, Ralph Loops, multi-agent PR reviewers, Workforce Productivity Matrix segmentation, role enablement.
- Stage 3 Value Realization (months 7–12): pillar Throughput, supporting Business outcome and ROI; unlocks L4–5 (HOTL); exit on AI Yield > 1.0 on top workflows with automatic demotion live. Moves: join telemetry to CRM/ERP/ITSM, pick top 3 workflows per function by value and CoD, promote ≥95% verified-success workflows to HOTL, move freed hours to strategic work.
- Stage 4 Continuous Excellence (months 13+): all five pillars; L6 for low-risk agents only; ongoing gate of quarterly total cost vs audited P&L; retire agents whose Verification Tax exceeds the time they save; scale high performers with burn-rate monitoring and compliance certification.
- Stage gates are computed from telemetry, not self-reported, and passed per team; a team is never gated on the organization average.
- Every agent gets a risk classification so stage ceiling + risk ceiling combine into a promotion plan (which level, at which stage, with what oversight).
- Fund the J-curve at organization level and track it per team: each team climbs out at its own pace, and the roll-up of the team curves is the organization's J-curve. Plan tuition capacity for the dip; optimize for net capacity (long-term) over gross generation speed (short-term).
- Wave plan: a wave is a group of teams that onboard together (~10–15 teams, ~30–50 people); each team then moves through its own team rollout stages, with security and governance reviewers sized to each wave.

**Primitives governed.** Team rollout stage, Stage gate, Five Pillars, Business outcome and ROI, Six domains, Tuition cost, Delivery J-curve, Risk classification.
