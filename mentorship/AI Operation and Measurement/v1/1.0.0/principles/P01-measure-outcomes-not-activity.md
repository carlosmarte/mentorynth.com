# P01 · Measure outcomes, not activity

**Statement.** Judge AI by the business outcomes it delivers that are actually accepted, per dollar of everything it costs to get them. Never by tokens used, users activated, prompts submitted, lines generated or gross hours saved.

**Why.** Traditional technology evaluations measure gross token generation or initial drafting speed ("tokenmaxxing") while ignoring the downstream cognitive, operational and architectural costs of validating probabilistic output. When stochastic models are dropped into deterministic workflows without telemetry, the time saved in generation is silently consumed by auditing, debugging and rework. Unchecked generation without matching review capacity only accumulates work in progress.

**Rules.**
- Activity metrics (active users, token consumption, lines, PRs/day) stay as leading-indicator SLIs. They are never key results.
- Key results are AI Yield, Net AI Value and Total Net Productivity Gain.
- Never reward lines or words generated (SPACE guardrail: Activity alone is a vanity metric).
- Rising agentic speed with falling stability is the "Amplified Illusion" and counts as a loss, not a gain.
- What the business funds is the net number, not usage.

**Primitives governed.** AI Yield, Net AI Value, Accepted Business Outcomes, Total Net Productivity Gain, Net Delivery Capacity, Production-Qualified Change, the AI Adoption SLI family (as leading indicators only).

**Related.** P03 (net not gross), P04 (only PQC counts), P07 (every number traces to an outcome).

**Source.** North Star · Framework Goals; AI Yield: Accepted Outcomes vs Total Cost; Callouts ("tokenmaxxing"); Leadership tier mandates ("The Right Adoption Metrics"); SPACE Activity guardrail.
